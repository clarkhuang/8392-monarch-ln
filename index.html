How the house flows

  • Split-foyer entry: the front door opens on a landing, with a short flight up to the main level and a longer flight down to the walkout.
  • Main level: kitchen at the back of the garage end with dining and a deck slider beside it, living room at the front, then a hall to the primary bedroom and bath in the back corner, a hall bath and two front bedrooms.
  • Lower level: a bright walkout family room across the back, a flex room or bedroom, a full bath and the utility room.
  • Garage: attached at the kitchen end; its floor sits about 4 ft below the main floor.

The refurbished vision

A light refresh of the main house and a complete redo of the outbuilding, in the same materials, lighting and palette as the 5312 Samels concept.

Main house refresh

  • New LVP flooring throughout, fresh paint and warm lighting
  • New baths: white-oak vanities, quartz, black fixtures, large-format tile, glass showers
  • Kitchen cabinets kept, new quartz tops
  • Nothing structural

Outbuilding fitness and recovery center

  • Guest kitchen, living room, bath and bedroom at one end
  • Recovery room: cedar sauna, cold plunge, massage chair, rinse shower
  • Open gym with the full home-gym setup plus high rings and a GHD
  • Glass overhead door opens the gym to the outside

Steinway piano lounge

The Steinway lives in the lower family room, the only room with real clearance around a 6′11″ case. It comes in through the walkout slider, with no stairs.

Airstream in the 3-car garage

The 2021 Airstream Interstate 24X (~24′4″ long) parks in the 28 × 27 garage with about 1.3 ft to spare at each end. The photos show door widths of about 16 ft and 9 ft; the door's clear height still needs measuring against the van's ~9′7″.

Renderings are a first pass: the gym equipment, massage chair and Airstream are simplified shapes for now.

Zoning and land

  • Residential zoning under the Whitewater Township ordinance (effective Feb 2025): a house, accessory buildings and animals for your own use are allowed.
  • Guest quarters: no provision for a second dwelling was found, so living in the outbuilding unit full-time needs confirming with the township. Short-term rentals aren't allowed by right in R-1.
  • Land: 5 open acres with a cherry orchard; the lot slopes to the rear, which creates the walkout.
  • Road: private maintained road off Elk Lake Rd. No special assessments or road projects found.
  • Utilities: private well and septic; propane.
  • Taxes (estimate): Michigan resets taxable value after a sale. At the 2025 rates (county millage) that's roughly $7,150/yr as a homestead.
